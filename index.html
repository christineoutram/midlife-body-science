Your hormones don't exist separately from your brain. Your pelvic floor doesn't exist separately from your nervous system. Your sleep doesn't exist separately from your stress. And your body doesn't exist separately from your life.

Neither should the way we understand women's health.

Midlife Body Science explores evidence-based women's health through a whole-person lens — connecting hormones, brain and nervous system, pelvic health, sleep, strength, pain, psychology, behavior, relationships and healthy aging.

We are interested in what changes in midlife, what actually helps, what is still poorly understood — and how the pieces connect.
